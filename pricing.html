FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Enterprise Pricing FAQ

Everything you need to know about retainers, invoicing, rollover terms, and SLA guarantees.

CONTRACT TERMS What is the minimum contract commitment period?
Starter and Business retainer plans are billed quarterly on a flexible 3-month rolling agreement. Enterprise custom contracts offer tailored 6-month or 12-month terms with annual prepayment discounts up to 20%.
ROLLOVER GUARANTEE Can unused quarterly unit allocations roll over?
Yes! Unused unit allocations on Business and Enterprise plans automatically roll over to the following quarter for up to 180 days, ensuring complete flexibility and zero waste of your corporate budget.
GST TAX CREDIT How does GST invoicing and tax credit work?
All Vanguard invoices include 18% GST with your company’s registered GSTIN clearly printed. Automated Input Tax Credit (ITC) compliant tax invoices are generated instantly in your client portal upon dispatch.
DESK DROP SLA How are multi-city desk drop shipments tracked?
Every shipment is tracked via real-time GPS telemetry from our 5 regional hubs across India. HR leads receive automated delivery notifications and digital recipient proof-of-delivery receipts.
